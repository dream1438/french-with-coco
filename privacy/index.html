Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains how Learn French with Coco (part of Languages with Coco) handles information on our website languageswithcoco.com and in our TikTok integration. Contact: languageswithcoco@gmail.com.

1. Our website

  • No accounts. You don't sign up or log in. We don't ask for your name or other personal details.
  • New-lesson emails (optional). If you type your email address in the "Get new lessons by email" box, it is stored by our email service, MailerLite (MailerLite Limited), only to send you an email when a new lesson is out. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and we delete your address when you unsubscribe or ask us to. MailerLite handles the address under its own privacy policy.
  • Your progress stays on your device. Lesson progress, quiz scores, XP, badges and flashcard reviews are saved only in your own browser (local storage). They are never sent to us. You can delete them at any time with "Reset my progress" or by clearing your browser data.
  • Microphone (optional). The "Speak with Coco" practice uses your browser's built-in speech recognition only when you press the microphone button. We don't record or store your voice. Depending on your browser, the audio may be processed by your browser's provider (for example Google for Chrome or Microsoft for Edge) under their own privacy policy.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking cookies. We don't use analytics or advertising tools.
  • Third-party content. Lesson videos are embedded from YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode, youtube-nocookie.com) and only load when you press play. The Arabic font loads from Google Fonts. Lesson words are played from audio files on this website. Worksheets open on Google Drive. These services may receive basic technical data such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs.

2. Our TikTok integration

We use TikTok Login Kit and the TikTok Content Posting API only to post our own course videos to our own TikTok account, @french.withcoco. It is not offered to the public and no other TikTok users sign in to it.

  • What we store: only the TikTok access token (and refresh token) and the basic profile information needed for posting: open ID and display name.
  • What we use it for: only to upload and publish our own lesson videos to @french.withcoco.
  • What we don't do: we don't read, collect or store any other TikTok user's data, and we don't access messages, followers or analytics beyond what posting requires.

3. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share personal data with anyone.

4. Your choices

  • You can revoke our app's access at any time in your TikTok settings (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services), or by emailing us. We then delete the stored token.
  • You can stop new-lesson emails at any time with the unsubscribe link in any email.
  • To ask a question or request deletion of any data, email languageswithcoco@gmail.com.

5. Children

Our lessons are suitable for all ages and the website collects no personal data, except an email address that someone chooses to give for new-lesson emails. If you are under 16, please ask a parent before signing up.

6. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above.
