Languages with Coco

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains how Learn French with Coco (part of Languages with Coco) handles information on our website languageswithcoco.com, in our French with Coco apps for Android and iPhone, and in our TikTok integration. Contact: hello@languageswithcoco.com.

1. Our website and apps

The apps are the same course as the website, so everything below applies to both.

  • Accounts are optional. You can use every lesson without an account. Then your progress (lessons, quiz scores, XP, streak, badges, flashcard reviews) is saved only on your device, and you can delete it with "Reset my progress", by clearing your browser data or by uninstalling the app.
  • If you create an account (with Google, Apple or an email sign-in link), we store your email address, your name and picture if your Google or Apple account shares them, and your course progress, so it syncs between the website and the apps. This is stored by our database provider, Supabase, and is used only to run your account. Apple's "Hide my email" addresses work too.
  • Delete your account any time. Signed in, open the account button (top of the screen) and choose Delete my account. Your account and all its progress are deleted at once and can't be restored. You can also ask us by email; see how to delete your account.
  • New-lesson emails (optional). If you type your email address in the "Get new lessons by email" box, it is stored by our email service, MailerLite (MailerLite Limited), only to send you an email when a new lesson is out. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and we delete your address when you unsubscribe or ask us to.
  • Microphone (optional). The "Speak with Coco" practice uses your device's built-in speech recognition only when you press the microphone button. We don't record or store your voice. Depending on your device, the audio may be processed by its provider (for example Google, Apple or Microsoft) under their own privacy policy.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking. We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, in the website or the apps.
  • Third-party content. Lesson videos are embedded from YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode, youtube-nocookie.com) and only load when you press play. The Arabic font loads from Google Fonts and the sign-in library from jsDelivr. Worksheets open on Google Drive. These services may receive basic technical data such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies. The website is hosted by Hostinger, which keeps standard server logs.

2. Our TikTok integration

We use TikTok Login Kit and the TikTok Content Posting API only to post our own course videos to our own TikTok account, @french.withcoco. It is not offered to the public and no other TikTok users sign in to it.

  • What we store: only the TikTok access token (and refresh token) and the basic profile information needed for posting: open ID and display name.
  • What we use it for: only to upload and publish our own lesson videos to @french.withcoco.
  • What we don't do: we don't read, collect or store any other TikTok user's data, and we don't access messages, followers or analytics beyond what posting requires.

3. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share personal data with anyone. The service providers named above only process it to run the service for us.

4. Your choices

  • You can revoke our app's access at any time in your TikTok settings (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services), or by emailing us. We then delete the stored token.
  • You can stop new-lesson emails at any time with the unsubscribe link in any email.
  • To ask a question or request deletion of any data, email hello@languageswithcoco.com.

5. Children

Our lessons are suitable for all ages and can be used without giving any personal data. Accounts and new-lesson emails are optional; if you are under 16, please ask a parent before creating an account or signing up for emails.

6. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above.
