Delete your account

French with Coco (website and Android / iPhone apps) · Languages with Coco

Accounts are optional. If you made one, you can delete it and all its data at any time.

In the app or on the website

  1. Open the app, or languageswithcoco.com/french, and sign in.
  2. Tap the account button at the top of the screen (your initial).
  3. Tap Delete my account, then confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

By email

Can't sign in any more? Email languageswithcoco@gmail.com from the address you used, with the subject "Delete my account". We delete it within 7 days and reply when it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address, and name or picture if Google or Apple shared them).
  • All your saved course progress: lessons, quiz scores, XP, streak, badges and flashcards.

Nothing is kept afterwards. Progress saved only on your own device (without an account) stays there until you reset it or uninstall the app. New-lesson emails are separate: use the unsubscribe link in any of them.
